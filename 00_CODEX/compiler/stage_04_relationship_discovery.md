# Stage 04: Relationship Discovery

## Purpose

Discover how concepts relate.

Relationship discovery turns isolated concepts into reusable intelligence by identifying how concepts affect, require, reinforce, or challenge each other.

## Relationship Types

- supports
- depends_on
- strengthens
- enables
- contradicts
- extends
- measures
- influences

## Allowed Work

- Identify relationships among concept candidates and canonical concepts.
- Record relationship type, direction, confidence, and evidence basis.
- Distinguish strong evidence from weak inference.
- Flag contradictory evidence.
- Prepare knowledge graph updates for review.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not create unsupported relationships.
- Do not flatten uncertain relationships into facts.
- Do not duplicate canonical concepts to express relationships.
- Do not modify evidence.
- Do not generate applications.

## Outputs

- Knowledge graph
- Relationship manifest
- Contradiction notes
- Relationship review queue

## Compiler Questions

- What concepts became stronger?
- What relationships became stronger?
- What relationships are contradicted?
- What canonical objects could improve from these relationships?
- Which applications should regenerate if the relationship is accepted?

## Exit Criteria

Concept relationships are explicit, evidence-backed, and ready to enrich canonical knowledge objects.

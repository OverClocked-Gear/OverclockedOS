# Stage 07: Output Publication

## Purpose

Produce audience-specific deliverables.

Output publication turns approved applications and canonical knowledge into final audience-facing materials. Outputs are downstream artifacts and should not become the source of canonical truth.

## Output Examples

- LinkedIn post
- Blog post
- Podcast outline
- White paper
- Presentation
- Lecture
- Case study
- Discussion prompt
- Executive brief
- Article

## Allowed Work

- Create audience-specific deliverables from canonical knowledge and approved applications.
- Tailor voice, length, format, and examples to the audience.
- Preserve citations, provenance, and instructor-only constraints.
- Identify outputs that must update when canonical knowledge changes.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not create new canonical definitions inside outputs.
- Do not publish unsupported claims.
- Do not expose proprietary, instructor-only, or assessment-secure material.
- Do not modify evidence.
- Do not treat public outputs as canonical sources.

## Outputs

- Published or publication-ready deliverables
- Output dependency notes
- Update recommendations
- Audience-specific variants

## Compiler Questions

- Which outputs should update?
- Which canonical objects does this output depend on?
- What evidence changed since the last output?
- What claims require citation or review?
- What audience constraints apply?

## Exit Criteria

Audience-specific outputs are generated from canonical knowledge, remain traceable, and can be updated when upstream knowledge changes.

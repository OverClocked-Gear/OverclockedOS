# Stage 03: Concept Extraction

## Purpose

Extract reusable ideas.

Concept extraction identifies human, team, organizational, ecosystem, and institutional ideas that may belong in the Adaptive Intelligence Layer. Concepts are candidates until evidence review and canonical checks are complete.

## Concept Examples

- Trust
- Psychological safety
- Dynamic capabilities
- Resource redeployment
- Institutional adaptation
- Learning velocity
- Human adaptation tax

## Allowed Work

- Identify recurring ideas in classified evidence.
- Create concept candidates.
- Map candidate concepts to supporting evidence.
- Identify research gaps.
- Compare candidates with existing Adaptive Intelligence concepts.
- Recommend whether an existing concept should be strengthened.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not create duplicate concepts.
- Do not treat one source as sufficient unless the evidence standard allows it.
- Do not modify evidence.
- Do not modify canonical objects unless the operation explicitly enters Stage 5.
- Do not generate applications.

## Outputs

- Concept candidates
- Evidence maps
- Research gaps
- Candidate-to-canonical comparison notes

## Compiler Questions

- What evidence changed?
- What concepts became stronger?
- What new concept candidates emerged?
- Which candidates duplicate or overlap existing concepts?
- What evidence is missing?

## Exit Criteria

Reusable ideas have been identified, evidence links are traceable, and candidates are ready for relationship discovery.

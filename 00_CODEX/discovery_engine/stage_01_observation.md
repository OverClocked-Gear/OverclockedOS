# Stage 01: Observation

## Purpose

Capture observations without interpretation.

Observation records what happened, what was observed, and what evidence exists. The Discovery Engine shall never treat an observation as truth. Observations are signals that may require classification, hypothesis generation, and validation.

## Examples

- Academic papers
- Industry reports
- Executive interviews
- Surveys
- Market behavior
- Government policy
- Financial reports
- Operational metrics

## Outputs

- Observation records
- Evidence metadata
- Research inventory
- Provenance notes
- Review flags

## Required Questions

- What happened?
- Who observed it?
- Where?
- When?
- What source or artifact records the observation?
- What is known directly versus inferred?

## Allowed Work

- Record observable metadata.
- Preserve provenance.
- Identify missing metadata.
- Create inventory and manifest records.
- Flag observations for classification.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not interpret meaning.
- Do not generate hypotheses.
- Do not create concepts.
- Do not modify evidence.
- Do not treat observations as validated knowledge.

## Exit Criteria

The observation is identifiable, traceable, and ready for classification.

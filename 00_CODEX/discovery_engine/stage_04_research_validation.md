# Stage 04: Research & Validation

## Purpose

Determine whether hypotheses are supported.

The Discovery Engine should recommend additional research whenever uncertainty remains. Validation is a structured process, not a naming exercise. Multiple mentions of similar language do not make a concept valid.

## Suggested Activities

- Literature review
- Meta-analysis
- Executive interviews
- Case studies
- Longitudinal research
- Experimental design
- Survey research
- Cross-industry comparison
- Devil's Advocate review
- Skeptic review
- Research Radar acquisition planning

## Outputs

- Validated
- Rejected
- Emerging
- Insufficient Evidence
- Research agenda
- Evidence strength
- Research gaps
- Devil's Advocate review
- Skeptic novelty assessment
- Research acquisition plan

## Required Questions

- What evidence supports the hypothesis?
- What evidence contradicts it?
- What alternative explanations could fit the same observations?
- Is this hypothesis novel, or does it restate existing theory?
- What methodology is appropriate?
- Is the evidence strong enough to explain behavior?
- What remains unknown?
- What external sources, datasets, interviews, or monitoring targets are needed?
- What additional research should be conducted?

## Allowed Work

- Assess evidence strength.
- Compare across sources, methods, industries, and contexts.
- Record contradictions.
- Conduct Devil's Advocate and Skeptic reviews.
- Use Research Radar to define acquisition targets.
- Recommend research methodologies.
- Update hypothesis status.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not accept hypotheses prematurely.
- Do not treat popularity as validation.
- Do not suppress contradictory evidence.
- Do not skip adversarial review.
- Do not ignore novelty or duplicate-risk concerns.
- Do not create concepts unless the hypothesis demonstrates explanatory value.

## Exit Criteria

The hypothesis has a clear research status, adversarial review record, novelty assessment, and enough evidence assessment to decide whether it can proceed to concept formation.

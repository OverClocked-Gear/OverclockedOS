# Stage 03: Hypothesis Generation

## Purpose

Generate plausible, testable hypotheses from observed evidence.

The Discovery Engine identifies recurring patterns, emerging signals, anomalies, and unexplained relationships. Every hypothesis is provisional until validated. Hypotheses shall never be presented as facts.

## Required Hypothesis Elements

- Hypothesis statement
- Reasoning
- Supporting observations
- Contradictory observations
- Confidence
- Research needed
- Potential impact
- Research priority
- Possible applications
- Status

## Status Rule

Every generated hypothesis must be explicitly labeled with a provisional status, usually `Hypothesis`, `Emerging`, or `Insufficient Evidence`. A hypothesis is not a concept and is not canonical knowledge.

## Example Pattern

Observation: Psychological safety appears repeatedly alongside organizational learning.

Candidate hypothesis: Organizations with higher levels of psychological safety reduce the Human Adaptation Tax, increasing learning velocity and organizational adaptability.

Status: Hypothesis. Not validated.

## Outputs

- Hypothesis objects
- Hypothesis clusters
- Research opportunities
- Research questions
- Devil's Advocate review queue
- Skeptic review queue
- Possible application notes

## Required Questions

- What appears to be recurring?
- What may explain the observed pattern?
- What would disconfirm this hypothesis?
- What evidence supports it?
- What evidence contradicts it?
- What research is needed next?

## Allowed Work

- Generate provisional, testable statements.
- Record reasoning transparently.
- Link to observations.
- Identify contradictory evidence.
- Route hypotheses to Devil's Advocate and Skeptic review before validation.
- Recommend research agendas.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not present hypotheses as facts.
- Do not convert hypotheses directly into concepts.
- Do not ignore contradictory observations.
- Do not bypass adversarial review.
- Do not generate applications from unvalidated hypotheses except as clearly marked exploratory ideas.

## Exit Criteria

The hypothesis is testable, provisional, evidence-linked, and queued for Devil's Advocate and Skeptic review before research and validation.

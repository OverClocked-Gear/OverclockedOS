# Stage 06: Relationship Discovery

## Purpose

Identify semantic relationships.

Relationship discovery explains how validated concepts, frameworks, capabilities, and canonical objects relate. Relationships must be directional, typed, and evidence-aware.

## Relationship Vocabulary

- supports
- extends
- contradicts
- depends_on
- strengthens
- enables
- influences
- measures
- instantiates
- belongs_to
- orchestrates
- transforms

## Outputs

- Knowledge graph
- Relationship maps
- Contradiction maps
- Relationship confidence notes

## Required Questions

- What relates to what?
- What is the relationship type?
- What is the direction of the relationship?
- What evidence supports the relationship?
- What evidence contradicts it?
- How confident is the relationship?

## Allowed Work

- Identify explicit and inferred relationships.
- Record confidence and evidence basis.
- Preserve contradictory relationships when evidence supports them.
- Recommend ontology enrichment.

## Prohibited Work

- Do not create unsupported relationships.
- Do not flatten uncertain relationships into facts.
- Do not duplicate concepts to express relationships.
- Do not hide contradictions.

## Exit Criteria

Relationships are explicit, typed, evidence-aware, and ready for canonical ontology enrichment.

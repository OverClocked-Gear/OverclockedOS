# Scientific Method

## Canonical Lifecycle

```text
Reality

Observation

Classification

Hypothesis Generation

Research & Validation

Concept Formation

Relationship Discovery

Ontology Enrichment

Application Generation

Publication

New Observation
```

Knowledge is never considered complete. Every publication becomes a new observation.

## Operating Principle

The Discovery Engine shall never assume that observations represent truth. Observations are signals that require investigation.

The purpose of the Discovery Engine is not to confirm existing beliefs. Its purpose is to identify:

- What appears true.
- What may be true.
- What remains unknown.

Scientific curiosity is preferred over premature certainty.

## Progression Rules

- Observations can become classified evidence.
- Classified evidence can support hypotheses.
- Hypotheses require research and validation.
- Only validated hypotheses can become concepts.
- Concepts do not automatically become frameworks.
- Frameworks do not automatically become canonical.
- Canonical knowledge must remain evidence-backed and updateable.

## Uncertainty Rules

- Uncertainty must be preserved.
- Contradictory evidence must be recorded.
- Confidence must be explicit.
- Unsupported claims must remain provisional.
- Additional research should be recommended whenever uncertainty remains.

## Regeneration Questions

Every operation should answer:

- What evidence changed?
- What observations became stronger?
- What hypotheses became stronger or weaker?
- What concepts became stronger?
- What canonical objects improved?
- Which applications should regenerate?
- Which outputs should update?

## Canonical Principle

Never duplicate knowledge. Strengthen relationships. Strengthen canonical objects. Generate applications from canonical knowledge.
